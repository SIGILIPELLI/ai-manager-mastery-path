# AI Manager Mastery Path

A structured, module-wise training program for **managing** AI initiatives,
teams, and products — from your first AI project to setting AI strategy at
the executive level. Every module ends with a real framework, template, or
worked example you can adapt directly, plus a hands-on exercise.

This track is **not** about hands-on ML engineering — you won't write model
training code here. It's about the management discipline around AI: scoping
projects realistically, leading data science and ML teams, evaluating vendors
and tools, managing risk and ethics, and communicating AI work to
non-technical stakeholders. If you want the hands-on technical track instead
— building and training models yourself — see
[AI/ML Mastery Path](https://sigilipelli.github.io/ai-ml-mastery-path/).

## How the program is organized

| Level | Focus | Modules |
|-------|-------|---------|
| [Level 1 · Entry](level-1/index.md) | What AI management is, project lifecycles, working with data science/ML teams, ethics and risk basics | 9 topics + 1 project |
| [Level 2 · Intermediate](level-2/index.md) | MLOps fundamentals, AI product metrics, data governance, cross-functional teams, procurement | 9 topics + 1 project |
| [Level 3 · Advanced](level-3/index.md) | Scaling AI teams, governance & compliance, LLM/GenAI initiatives, talent strategy, ROI | 9 topics + 1 project |
| [Level 4 · Master](level-4/index.md) | Executive AI strategy, org design, portfolio management, board reporting, career growth | 9 topics + 1 capstone |

## How to use this site

- Work through each level in order — later modules assume earlier ones.
- Every topic page includes a concrete framework or template (formatted as a
  table or structured example, not code), plus a worked real-world example.
- Each topic ends with an **Exercise** — a practical deliverable to produce
  using your own project or team as the subject.
- Each level ends with a project that combines everything learned in that
  level into a real document: a charter, an MLOps process design, a
  governance framework, or a full strategy document.
- Use the search bar (top of the page) to jump straight to a topic.

Start here → [Level 1 · Entry](level-1/index.md)

## Who this is for

Engineering managers moving into AI/ML team leadership, product managers
taking on AI features, technical program managers coordinating AI
initiatives, and anyone who needs to plan, staff, budget, or govern AI work
without necessarily writing the models themselves.
