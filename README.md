# AI Manager Mastery Path

A free, structured, module-wise AI management training program — entry level
to master level, with real frameworks, templates, and worked examples in
every module. Focused on **managing** AI initiatives, teams, and products,
not hands-on ML engineering (see
[AI/ML Mastery Path](https://sigilipelli.github.io/ai-ml-mastery-path/) for
the technical track).

**Live site:** https://sigilipelli.github.io/ai-manager-mastery-path/

## Contents

- **Level 1 · Entry** — what AI management is, AI/ML fundamentals for
  managers, project lifecycles, managing data science/ML teams, ethics and
  risk basics, vendor evaluation, stakeholder communication, and a project
  charter + risk assessment capstone
- **Level 2 · Intermediate** — ML experimentation management, AI product
  metrics, MLOps fundamentals, data governance, cross-functional teams,
  procurement, responsible AI frameworks, change management, budgeting
- **Level 3 · Advanced** — scaling AI teams, governance & compliance,
  LLM/GenAI initiatives, talent strategy, platform strategy, incident
  management, ROI measurement, vendor management, centers of excellence
- **Level 4 · Master** — executive AI strategy, org design, portfolio
  management, leading through disruption, regulation & policy, board
  reporting, AI culture, M&A integration, career growth to Chief AI Officer
